      Christmas recipe ideas

      Christmas recipes

      Ideas for your holiday table

      Classic, easy dishes that let the produce shine. Tap the button and we'll add it to your Christmas order.

      Starter · 10 min

      Boiled prawns

      Very salty water (about 60 g per litre). When it boils, add the prawns and, once it comes back to the boil, count 1-2 minutes depending on size. Move them to iced salted water so they stay firm.

      Oven · 45 min

      Baked sea bream

      Make a bed of sliced potato and onion and bake it for 20 minutes. Lay the bream on top with a few lemon slices and give it 20-25 more minutes at 180 °C. Finish with garlic and chilli fried in oil with a dash of vinegar.

      Main · 40 min

      Stuffed hake

      Fill a butterflied hake with prawns, mushrooms and a few breadcrumbs. Close it, splash with white wine and bake at 180 °C for 20-25 minutes. We'll butterfly and bone it for you.

      Tapa · 5 min

      Grilled razor clams

      Rinse them well, use a very hot griddle and take them off as soon as they open (1-2 minutes). Drizzle with oil, chopped garlic and parsley and serve.
